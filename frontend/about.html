iKhethelo Digital is a Durban-based web and software development studio building websites, applications and business systems with clear purpose.

The name

A name connected to choosing.

iKhethelo Digital is inspired by the Nguni root "Khetha," meaning "to choose" or "to select." More deeply, iKhethelo represents something exceptionally chosen—selected with purpose, intention, and distinction.

The name also carries a personal connection to our founder, Khethelo Machi, reflecting a philosophy of purpose, quality, and meaningful work. The lowercase "i" gives the brand a modern digital identity, while Digital reflects the creativity, technical expertise, and dedication behind everything we build.

Engineering Experience

iKhethelo Digital is founded on approximately five years of professional full-stack software engineering experience, building, deploying, and maintaining enterprise web applications using modern Microsoft technologies.

Our experience spans front-end development, back-end services, REST APIs, database design, production support, and Agile software delivery. Every solution is built with a strong focus on clean architecture, maintainability, and long-term reliability.
